About

REVERB is UC Berkeley's hub for electronic music culture. This is a community where students dive deep into DJing, production, and the music industry. REVERB teaches and empowers students to turn their passion for music into creative skills. From hands-on workshops to music showcases, we're here to amplify your sound and continue building Berkeley's electronic music scene from the ground up.
